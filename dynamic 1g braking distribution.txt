## weight transfer params

### mass

M = total mass of car
G = weight of car

G = M * g

GF = total weight on front wheels induced by mass
GB = weight on back wheel induced by mass

GF + GB = G

### lengths

LA = length between axles

LF = distance from CoG to front wheel axle
LB = distance from CoG to back wheel 

LF + LB = LA

LC = height of CoG

## braking 

A = braking deacceleration
Mu = tire grip coefficient

### braking force setup

FT = braking force total = M * A

FF = braking force on front
FB = braking force on rear

### decel induced weight

IF = weight on front wheels induced by weight
IB = weight on back wheel induced by weight

total torque is 0

FT * LC = LT * IF + LB * IB

### total weight

TF = GF + IF
TB = GB - IB

### braking force

FF = TF / G * FT
FB = TB / G * FT
